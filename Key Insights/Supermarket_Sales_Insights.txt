# Key Insights

## 1. Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard provides an overall view of supermarket performance using key metrics such as total revenue, total units sold, total transactions, average unit price, and total tax.

## 2. City-Wise Revenue

Revenue varies across the three cities shown in the dashboard. **Yangon** has the highest revenue, followed by **Naypyitaw** and **Mandalay**.

## 3. Product Performance

Revenue and quantity are compared across different product lines. **Food and Beverages** is identified as the top product line in the Business Performance Dashboard.

## 4. Revenue by Gender

The dashboard compares revenue between male and female customers, providing a view of sales contribution by gender.

## 5. Branch Performance

The business performance dashboard compares revenue across Branches A, B, and C. **Branch C** is identified as the top branch in the dashboard.

## 6. Customer Type

Revenue is compared between **Member** and **Normal** customers. The dashboard shows a nearly balanced distribution between the two customer types.

## 7. Tax Analysis

Tax is analyzed by product line and month. The dashboard allows comparison of tax contributions across different products and monthly periods.

## 8. Quantity and Unit Price

The dashboard compares total quantity sold and unit prices across product categories, helping identify differences in sales volume and pricing.

## 9. Interactive Analysis

The dashboard includes filters for **Year, Branch, Customer Type, Gender, and City**, allowing users to interactively explore different segments of the supermarket sales data.

## Overall Insight

The project provides a consolidated view of supermarket sales performance by combining revenue, transactions, products, customers, branches, cities, quantity, pricing, and tax analysis in interactive Excel dashboards.
